Every form has two tabs for understanding its responses: **Submissions** for record-level review and **Analytics** ("Performance Insights") for trends. Open a form in the builder at `/dpdpc/<org-slug>/forms/<form-id>/edit` and pick a tab.

## Submissions tab

The toolbar shows **Total Submissions**, the **Latest Entry** date, and an entry count.

- **Search** — free-text search across submission IDs, respondent identities, and every answer value.
- **Sort** — click a column header to sort ascending, click again for descending, a third time to remove sorting.
- **Columns** — click **Columns** to choose which question columns are visible. Use **All**, **None**, or **Reset** (which restores the default first six question fields).
- **Row actions** — open the detail drawer, view the full signed-submission PDF, or delete the entry (a confirmation dialog warns the action cannot be undone).

### Submission details drawer

Each drawer shows the submission's status (**Recorded**), date, respondent (or Anonymous Respondent), and every answer as its own card. Highlights:

- **Official Submission & Signed Agreement PDF** — a complete record with responses, agreed terms, and signature. Use **View Full PDF**.
- **First-Party Attribution (DPDP 2023)** — cookie-free traffic source, campaign, device category, and submission duration, when available.
- **Consents** — a summary such as "2 of 3 Consents Accepted" with one row per purpose.
- **Signatures and attachments** — view-only, protected previews; files cannot be downloaded from the drawer.

### Export

Click **Export** for **Export to CSV (.csv)** or **Export to JSON (.json)**. Exports download immediately and cover **all** responses for the form — not just the rows matching your current search. Export is disabled until at least one response exists.

## Analytics tab

<Note>
Analytics are 100% first-party and cookie-free (DPDP Act 2023). Location data is country-level, without raw IP logging or tracking cookies.
</Note>

1. Pick a **Time Range**: All Time (default), Today, 7 Days, 30 Days, 4 Weeks, 3 Months — or choose a custom range from the calendar and **Apply**.
2. Read the KPI cards:
   - **Total Views** — form page impressions.
   - **Submissions** — completed responses.
   - **Top Origin Country** — leading country with its share of traffic.
   - **Conversion Rate** — submissions divided by views.
3. **From Location** breaks down views, submissions, and conversion rate per country.
4. **Activity Timeline** plots views and submissions per day; hover a bar for exact counts.
