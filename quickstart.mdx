This guide walks you through deploying DPDP-compliant consent capture and privacy vault storage for your organization.

## Prerequisites

Before getting started, make sure you have:
- An active Data Fiduciary account on `dpdpcomply`.
- Designated your organization's **Data Protection Officer (DPO)** or privacy contact details.
- Identified the campus or operational branch where data will be collected.

---

## 4-Step Quickstart

<Steps>
  <Step title="Configure your organization">
    Log in to your dashboard and open **Settings** > **Organization Details**. Set your institution's legal name, address, and contact details, and upload your logo. Then add each campus under **Branches**, and name your grievance officer under **Settings** > **Grievance Officer**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a consent policy">
    Go to **DPDP Consent Policy** > **Create Consent Policy**.
    1. Pick a preset (for example, Student Admission & Enrollment Consent) or start from scratch.
    2. List every purpose you collect data for — the first is your required core service, the rest are optional.
    3. Add your regional language so the notice is available in English and your language.
    4. Click **Bump & Publish** to make the policy ready for your forms.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Publish a form with consent">
    Open **Forms** > **Create form** (blank or from a template). Add your questions, then add a **Consent Toggles** field and click **Attach Organization Policy** to link the policy from Step 2. Click **Publish** — your shareable link, QR code, and embed code are ready instantly.
  </Step>

  <Step title="See the responses arrive">
    Submit a test response through your form's public link. It appears under the form's **Submissions** tab and in **Data Submissions**, and respondents receive a consent receipt by email with a link to withdraw consent at any time.
  </Step>
</Steps>
