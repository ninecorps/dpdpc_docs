This guide walks you through deploying DPDP-compliant consent capture and privacy vault storage for your organization.

## Prerequisites

Before getting started, make sure you have:
- An active Data Fiduciary account on `dpdpcomply`.
- Designated your organization's **Data Protection Officer (DPO)** or privacy contact details.
- Identified the campus or operational branch where data will be collected.

---

## 4-Step Quickstart

<Steps>
  <Step title="Configure Organization & Campus">
    Log in to your dashboard and navigate to **Settings** > **Organization Profile**. Set your institution's statutory legal name, official address, and DPO email address.
    
    If managing multiple schools or branches, add them under **Branches** to enable campus-level vault isolation.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create an Unbundled Privacy Notice">
    Go to **Terms & Policy** > **New Notice**.
    1. Select the processing purpose (e.g., `STUDENT_ADMISSIONS`, `PARENT_COMMUNICATION`).
    2. Specify the data fields collected (e.g., Full Name, DOB, Parent Mobile, Aadhaar Last-4).
    3. Choose supported languages (e.g., English, Hindi, Telugu).
    4. Click **Publish Notice** to generate a versioned notice hash.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Publish a Form with Consent Modal">
    Open **Forms** > **New Form**. Add your input fields and toggle **Attach Privacy Notice**.
    
    Select the notice created in Step 2. The form runtime will enforce unbundled consent and generate signed **Consent Artifacts** upon submission.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Inspect Verified Vault Records">
    Submit a test form response. Head to **Privacy Vault** in your dashboard to verify:
    - Personal identifiers are encrypted and tokenized.
    - A cryptographic **Consent Artifact** is generated with SHA-256 signature proof.
  </Step>
</Steps>
