When you sign in for the first time (or when your account has no organization yet), dpdpcomply routes you to a three-step setup wizard: **Organization**, **Plan selection**, and **Checkout**. Completing it creates your organization and opens your workspace home.

<Note>
You can also open the wizard directly at `/dpdpc/onboarding/create-org`. If you already manage an organization, the dashboard redirects you home instead.
</Note>

## Create your organization

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your organization details">
    On the **Create your organization** page, fill in:

    - **Organization name** — your institution's name. The workspace URL slug auto-fills from it.
    - **Workspace URL Slug** — used in every workspace URL (`/dpdpc/<slug>/...`). Lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens only.
    - **Timezone** and **Currency** — pre-filled from your profile or browser where possible. Indian Rupee (INR) is the default currency.
    - **Additional details (optional)** — for example, your primary campus address or board affiliation.

    Click **Continue to Plan Selection**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Pick a billing interval first: **Monthly billing** or **Annual billing** (annual saves 15%). Then choose:

    - **Essentials** — one campus, core statutory compliance.
    - **Professional** — adds a custom domain, audit packs, re-consent campaigns, and the website tracker scanner.
    - **Group** (*Most popular*) — multi-campus trusts: consolidated roll-up, campus isolation, bulk provisioning.
    - **Enterprise** — SSO, dedicated deployment, and API access. Selecting it opens a **Contact sales** action instead of self-serve checkout.

    Each card shows the per-student rate, minimum enrolment, campus count, admin seats, and support SLA. Use **Compare our plans** for the full side-by-side table.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete checkout">
    The checkout step finalizes your subscription. In the current release, online payment is not available yet: the page shows **Contact sales to activate a plan** and takes no payment.

    <Tip>
      Plan prices shown in the app come from the live plan catalog and can differ from marketing material — always rely on the checkout summary for the final amount.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After your organization is active

Once your organization is provisioned, you land in your workspace at `/dpdpc/<your-slug>/home`. The URL slug you chose becomes part of every workspace link, and your account becomes the organization's first administrator.
