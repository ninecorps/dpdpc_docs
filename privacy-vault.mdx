The **Privacy Vault** is a secure, isolated data store built on zero-trust principles. Instead of storing identifiable personal data across distributed application databases, sensitive data is tokenized and stored in dedicated vaults.

## Vault Capabilities

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Tenant & Campus Isolation" icon="layer-group">
    Each organization and branch/campus operates in an encrypted, logically isolated vault partition with dedicated key management.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Field-Level Tokenization" icon="key">
    Personal identifiers (Aadhaar last-4, phone numbers, addresses, parent details) are replaced by synthetic surrogate tokens throughout client applications.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Nominee Management" icon="user-group">
    Enables Data Principals to register nominees who can exercise statutory rights in the event of death or incapacity (Section 14).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Automated Erasure Engine" icon="trash-can">
    When consent is withdrawn or the retention purpose expires (Section 8(7)), the vault performs cryptographic erasure or token rotation.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Nominee Claim Workflow

Section 14 of the DPDP Act provides Data Principals the right to nominate any individual to exercise their rights in case of death or incapacity.

1. **Nomination**: Captured securely during onboarding with dual-factor verification.
2. **Claim Intake**: The nominee initiates a claim via the public portal using their mobile number and certificate evidence.
3. **Verification**: Organization DPO reviews documentation and approves transfer of rights.
4. **Vault Delegation**: Vault access tokens are regenerated and delegated to the verified nominee.
