The **Domains** page (`/dpdpc/<org-slug>/domains`) manages everything tied to your institution's web domain: ownership verification, the Section 9(3) tracker and pixel scanner, custom email sending, and branded subdomain portals.

<Note>
The Domains area requires the domain-management permission. If you don't see it in the sidebar, ask a head-office administrator.
</Note>

## Add and verify a domain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register the domain">
    Click **Add domain** and enter your institution's official website domain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the DNS TXT record">
    The **Required DNS TXT Record** panel shows the exact record to create at your DNS provider (Cloudflare, Route53, GoDaddy, and similar). Copy the host and value from the panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    Click **Verify Ownership (Live DNS)**. The page queries public DNS and shows a **Live DNS Resolution Report** with per-host status — for example a **Double-Domain** chip means your provider appended the domain twice and you should enter only the host prefix.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Verification reads public DNS, which can cache for several minutes after a change. If the record was just added, re-check after the cache window shown on the page.
</Tip>

## Scan for trackers and pixels

The **Tracker & Pixel Scanner** tab audits your website for advertising pixels (Meta, Google Ads), behavioural recorders (Hotjar), and cookies, under DPDP Act 2023 Section 9(3):

1. Run **Quick Scan (15 pages)** or **Deep Scan (35+ pages)**. Scanning requires verified ownership.
2. Read the verdict banner: compliant with zero tracking trackers, or non-compliant with the detected pixels.
3. Review findings — each lists the tracker, severity, category, page, evidence, and the Section 9(3) violation note. Filter by category or severity.
4. Click **Download Audit Dossier (PDF)** for the compliance report.

## Set up email sending

The **Email Sending** tab provisions a custom outbound sender on your domain for consent receipts, OTP codes, and statutory notices:

1. Enable an address (the suggestion defaults to `care@<your-domain>`).
2. Add the **Email Deliverability DNS Records** shown — DKIM CNAME records plus a DMARC TXT record (DMARC is optional and does not block verification).
3. Click **Re-check DNS** until the records verify, then use **Send Test Email** to confirm delivery.

Use **Disconnect Email** to revert to dpdpcomply's default sending identity.

## Publish branded portals

The **Branded Subdomains** tab publishes two portal types under your domain:

- **Forms Portal** (`forms.<domain>`) — public directory of your live forms.
- **DPDP Vault** (`dpdpvault.<domain>`) — the data principal privacy vault.

Add the CNAME and verification TXT records shown on each row, then **Verify DNS (Live)**. Renaming a portal prefix changes its hostname and DNS records — the portal returns to Pending DNS until verified again.

## Remove a domain

Deleting a domain is permanent and immediate: verification records, all scanner runs and PDF dossiers, the email identity, and every branded subdomain are purged, and public services stop.
