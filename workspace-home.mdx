Workspace home is your landing page after sign-in, at `/dpdpc/<org-slug>/home`. It summarizes your DPDP fiduciary posture and gives you one-click access to the day's compliance work.

## Choose a scope

The dashboard works in two scopes, controlled by the organization switcher in the header:

- **All Branches** — a consolidated view across every campus (org-wide users only).
- **A single branch** — data for one campus. If your account is scoped to a single branch, you are pinned to it automatically.

To switch scope, click the organization name in the header, then pick **All Branches Overview** or a branch row. From the same panel you can copy the **Org ID** or open **Manage Organization**.

## Dashboard tab

- **Welcome banner** — appears while your workspace has no data yet, with a **Create first form** button and a role-based shortcut (organization configuration, branch team management, or branch details).
- **DPDP Fiduciary Posture bar** — your posture status with quick actions: **Create Form**, **Rights Inbox** (shows a badge for pending statutory requests), and **Vault Explorer** (opens the Privacy Vault portal in a new tab).
- **KPI cards**:
  - **Verified Consents** — total verified consents across your forms, with an Adult vs Minor (Sec 9) split.
  - **Pending SLA Actions** — how many statutory rights requests are resolved versus pending, with a link to the SLA inbox.
  - **Active Consent Notices** — live consent forms in production, plus how many are still in draft.
  - **Cryptographic Vault** — vault status with a link to inspect it.
- **Consent Notices & Forms** — your four most recent forms with Live/Draft status and response counts. Use **View all** to open Forms.
- **Fiduciary SLA Queue** — pending Section 11 & 12 rights requests and grievances with a **Triage** link per item.
- **Recent Verified Submissions & Consent Receipts** — the latest submissions with the data principal, form, subject category (Adult or Minor/Child, Sec 9), consent proof, and date.

<Note>
Some figures — the posture pill and vault status line — are fixed status labels, not live measurements. Treat the KPI numbers and the SLA queue as the source of truth.
</Note>

## Getting Started tab

A four-step checklist that mirrors a compliant setup sequence:

1. **Organization Profile & Fiduciary Identity** — **Configure details**
2. **DPDP Terms & Statutory Notices** — **Manage terms**
3. **Consent Collection Intake Forms** — **Create form**
4. **Fiduciary Vault & Grievance Redressal** — **Open vault**

Each card links straight to the relevant part of the workspace.

## Recent Updates tab

An activity feed area for form submissions, consent updates, and policy revisions. Until activity streaming is enabled it shows the placeholder **No recent activity**.
