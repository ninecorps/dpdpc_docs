The **Vendors** page (`/dpdpc/<org-slug>/vendors`) is your register of vendor companies — transport providers, EdTech tools, payment processors, and similar — with their statutory data processor agreements (DPAs) and compliance records.

The DPDP role you record matters across the product:

- **Processor (acts on your behalf)** — you must notify them to stop processing or erase data when a principal withdraws or requests erasure.
- **Independent fiduciary (own consent)** — they collect data under their own consent; principals contact them directly.

Registered vendors can be linked to individual forms in the form's Integrations tab — see [Form integrations](/form-integrations).

## Add a vendor

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Click **Add Vendor**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill the company profile">
    Company name (required), category, website, city and state, GSTIN, lifecycle, annual spend, and tags; plus a **Primary Contact** (name, email, phone).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the DPDP role">
    Choose **Processor** or **Independent fiduciary**, and set the notice email if different from the contact email. Describe what the vendor does for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach files (optional)">
    Drag in MSA, certificates, or KYC documents (PDF, Word, Excel, or images, up to 3.5 MB each), then **Add vendor**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage a vendor

Click a row to open the detail drawer:

- **Overview** — contract terms (end date, reference, annual spend), which linked forms receive data, and profile fields.
- **Contacts** — add and remove account contacts.
- **Activity** — a timeline of notes and call logs.
- **Docs** — attach, download, rename, or replace documents.

Use **Edit profile** to update details, or **Upload Contract Copy** to record the signed agreement with its end date, reference, and value.

<Warning>
**Archive** from a vendor's row menu takes effect immediately, without a confirmation dialog — the vendor disappears from the list.
</Warning>
