The **Integrations** tab of a form (in the builder at `/dpdpc/<org-slug>/forms/<form-id>/edit`) connects submissions to spreadsheet destinations and tracks which vendors receive the data.

<Warning>
Per the in-app notice: active spreadsheet connectors and outgoing webhooks are automatically tracked as authorized third-party data processors in your Data Processing Lineage (DPL) and privacy vaults. Ensure a valid Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is executed with each external recipient under Section 8(1).
</Warning>

## Spreadsheet destinations

Three connectors are available. Changes stage locally — click **Save Changes** on the Integrations page to apply them.

### Zoho Sheet (one-click OAuth)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the connector">
    Click **Connect Spreadsheet** on the Zoho Sheet card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize Zoho">
    Click **Connect Zoho Sheet** — a Zoho sign-in popup opens (allow popups). dpdpcomply requests Zoho Sheet and WorkDrive permissions, then creates the workbook and maps columns automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure and save">
    Set the **Spreadsheet (Workbook) Name** (default: `{form name} Responses`) and **Worksheet (Tab) Name** (default: Form Responses). Review the auto-configured column list, then **Save & Enable Sync** — and **Save Changes** on the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use **Disconnect** to remove the Zoho account connection.

### Google Sheets and Microsoft Excel (webhook URL)

These connectors post each submission to an endpoint you create on the Google or Microsoft side (an Apps Script web app URL for Google Sheets, or a Power Automate / webhook URL for Excel):

1. Create the destination script/flow on the provider's side and copy its URL.
2. Paste it into **Google Apps Script Web App URL** or **Power Automate / Webhook URL**.
3. Optionally set **Spreadsheet Name** and **Worksheet Tab Name** (reference only — delivery depends on your endpoint).
4. Click **Test Connection** to verify the endpoint — a success shows the round-trip time.
5. Click **Save & Connect**, then **Save Changes**.

Use the per-card toggle to pause a connector, and **Save Changes** to apply.

## Vendors receiving this data

Tick each vendor (from your Vendors register) that receives this form's data, optionally **Limit to purpose**, then **Save vendor links**. Processors act on your behalf — dpdpcomply records withdrawals and erasures and helps you notify them. Independent fiduciaries collect data under their own consent, so principals contact them directly. See [Vendors](/vendors).
