The **Forms** page (`/dpdpc/<org-slug>/forms`) is your dashboard for every data-collection form. Each form is privacy-governed: fields carry Personal/Encrypted privacy labels, and legal building blocks (terms, signature, DPDP consent toggles) are built in.

## Forms list

- **Filter chips** — All forms, Live, Drafts, Closed, each with a count.
- **Search** — matches form name, purpose, and target group.
- **Per-card info** — status badge (Live, Draft, Closed, Disabled), entries count, audience, last updated date, and the public link for live forms.

## Create a form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start from blank or a template">
    Click **Create form** for a blank form, or **Browse templates** to open the **Form Templates Gallery**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a template (optional)">
    Search or filter the gallery by consent type — Parental, Lawful Guardian / PwD, Self 18+ — and click **Preview** to open a full-page preview, or **Use Template** to create a form from it right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build it">
    A new form opens in the form builder. See [Form builder](/form-builder) for fields, pages, and consent configuration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Template gallery

There are 13 prebuilt templates, each compliant with DPDP parental and self-consent frameworks:

- **Admission Form**, **Exam Registration** (Admissions)
- **Transport Registration** (Logistics)
- **Hostel Admission** (Residential Life)
- **Placement Registration**, **Staff Onboarding** (Human Resources)
- **Scholarship Application** (Financial Aid)
- **Field Trip Consent** (Activities & Events)
- **Photo / Media Consent** (Media & Communications)
- **EdTech Platform Consent**, **Pre-Existing Student Roster Consent (Sec 5(2))** (Technology & Compliance)
- **PwD Accommodation & Support Form** (Special Needs & Inclusion)
- **Student Health & Infirmary Emergency Consent** (Health & Safety)

Cards show a consent badge (for example **Parent + guardian**, **Self consent (18+)**), statutory badges such as **RPwD / Rule 11** or **Encrypted vault**, and how many questions the template asks.

## Manage existing forms

Open the **⋮** menu on any form card:

- **Edit in builder** — open the form builder.
- **Submissions & entries** / **Analytics & trends** — jump to that tab.
- **Integrations & webhooks** — connect the form to sheets, webhooks, or email.
- **Duplicate form** — copies the form under a new name (it does not open the builder).
- **Delete form** — permanently removes the form after a confirmation dialog. This cannot be undone.

Use the **Accepting entries / Entries paused** switch to open or pause public access. Pausing reverts the form to draft: the public link stops accepting submissions until you open access again.

<Tip>
Click the public-link snippet on a live card to copy the respondent URL to your clipboard.
</Tip>

<Note>
If your plan's form quota is full, a **Form limit reached** dialog appears when you try to create. Use **View plans** to open Billing and upgrade.
</Note>
