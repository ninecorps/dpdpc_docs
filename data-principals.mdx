The **Data Principals** page (`/dpdpc/<org-slug>/profiles`) is a consolidated directory of every individual across your forms — with cross-form consent ledgers and Section 9 minors flagged.

## Search the registry

- **Search** — by name, email, or phone.
- **Classification** — All Age Types, Adults Only, or Minors / Children.
- **Status** — All Statuses, 100% Consented, Has Withdrawn Consent, or Pending SLA Request.
- **Columns** — classification (Adult Principal or Minor/Child, Sec 9), ingestion (forms), consent ledger (granted vs total), fiduciary SLA state, and last activity. Page size is 25.

KPI cards at the top summarize total principals, adults, minors, and pending rights requests.

## Open a 360° dossier

Click **Inspect** on a row to open the dossier drawer with five tabs:

- **Attributes & PII** — every captured field grouped into identity & contact, sensitive personal data, documents & signatures, and residence, with the purpose, lawful basis, and retention for each.
- **Submissions** — every form response linked to this person, with per-purpose consent badges.
- **Consent Ledger** — the statutory purpose-by-purpose record under Rule 3: the Section 5 notice text, granular purpose consents, the affirmative declaration, and attached terms with signatures.
- **Fiduciary SLA** — this person's correction, deletion, and grievance requests.
- **Data Processing Lineage** — retention, hosting/encryption, and cross-border processing details.

## Export and erase

- **Export Registry** — downloads the current page (up to 25 profiles) as CSV.
- **Export Dossier** — downloads a full JSON dossier for one principal.
- **Delete Profile** — permanently purges the principal, their submissions, attachments, and consent ledger entries, behind a confirmation dialog with the Section 12 statutory notice. This cannot be reversed.
