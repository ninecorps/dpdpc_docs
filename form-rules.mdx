The **Rules** tab of a form (in the builder) has three rule builders. Rules apply to the live form once the form is saved and published.

## Field rules

Show or hide a field based on respondent input.

1. Open **Field Rules** and click **Configure Now** (or **Add Rule** when rules exist).
2. Build the condition: **When** [field] [operator] [value]. Operators include equals, does not equal, contains, is empty, is not empty, is less than, and is greater than or equal to. Combine conditions with **AND** / **OR**, and use **Add Condition** for more.
3. Set the **Action** to **Show** or **Hide** and pick the **Target Field**.
4. Use the card's switch to disable a rule without deleting it.

<Note>
Fields hidden by rules are exempt from required-field validation — respondents are never blocked by a field they cannot see.
</Note>

## Choice-based field rules

Same condition builder, but for the options *inside* a choice field (dropdowns, radios, checkboxes): choose a choice-type field as the target, then tick the specific **Target Choices** to show or hide.

## Form rules (email on submission)

Send the respondent (or another inbox) a complete PDF of their submission:

1. Open **Form Rules** and click **Configure Rule**. A new **Email Rule** is created, Active by default.
2. Choose the recipient:
   - **Form Email Field** — an email field in your form (the respondent gets the email).
   - **Custom Email** — one or more comma-separated addresses.
   - **Both** — respondent and custom recipients.
3. Edit the **Email Subject** (leave blank for the default `Submission Received: {form name}`) and the **Email Message**.
4. Keep **Attach complete form submission as PDF** ticked to generate an official PDF with the form title, metadata, and all answers.

Duplicate, disable, or remove a rule from the icons on its card.
