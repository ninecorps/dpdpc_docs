Consent under Section 6 of the DPDP Act must be **free, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous**, demonstrated through clear affirmative action. `dpdpcomply` automates the entire consent lifecycle from initial notice presentation to verifiable revocation.

## Consent Lifecycle Stages

<Steps>
  <Step title="Notice Presentation & Language Selection">
    Present unbundled notices specifying the exact personal data collected, specific processing purposes, rights of the Data Principal, and contact information of the Data Protection Officer.
    
    <Note>
      Supports English and all 22 Eighth Schedule Indian languages (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali, Marathi, etc.) with automatic locale fallback.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Affirmative Explicit Capture">
    Capture explicit, positive consent actions. Pre-ticked boxes, bundled terms, or implied agreements are strictly blocked by the form runtime.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cryptographic Consent Artifact Generation">
    On submission, the system generates a tamper-proof JSON-LD **Consent Artifact** signed with SHA-256 and private key attestation, capturing:
    - Principal identifier hash
    - Purpose codes and specific data field mappings
    - Exact notice version and text hash agreed upon
    - Timestamp (UTC + IST ISO 8601) and capture vector (IP hash, user-agent, session ID)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ongoing Governance & Easy Withdrawal">
    Data Principals can view their active consents via the self-service portal or SMS/email link and withdraw consent with the same ease as granting it (Section 6(4)).
  </Step>
</Steps>
