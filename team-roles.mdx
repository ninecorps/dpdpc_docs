**Team & Roles** (`/dpdpc/<org-slug>/team`) manages who can access your workspace, what they can do, and which branches they see.

<Note>
Branch head officers see a scoped view: they can invite into the branches they lead but not manage organization-wide members. Without the user-management permission the page is hidden entirely.
</Note>

## Invite a member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invite dialog">
    Click **Invite member** (disabled when your plan's admin seats are fully used).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill the details">
    Full name (optional), email, a role from your organization's role catalog, and a campus — leave campus empty for **All campuses**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    The member receives an email with a link to create their account. Pending invitations appear below with their expiry date, where you can **Resend** (old link stops working) or **Revoke invite** (the reserved seat is released).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage members

From a member row's menu:

- **Change role or campus** — adjust roles or move them to a specific campus (or back to All campuses).
- **Make head officer of `{campus}`** — assign the branch head officer for a campus; **Remove as branch head officer** undoes it.
- **Remove member** — they lose access and their seat is freed for a new invite.

The header badge shows seat usage (for example `3/4 seats`). When every included seat is allocated, the page explains your options: remove a member or upgrade your tier — **View plans** opens Billing.
