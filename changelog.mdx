<Update label="2026-11-01" description="v1.1.0 — Clean MVP Release" tags={["MVP", "Core"]}>
  ## DPDP Rules 2025 Hardening & Audit Packs

  - **Board-Ready Audit Packs**: Fiduciaries can now generate signed audit bundles containing Consent Artifacts, notice version proofs, and vault access logs.
  - **CERT-In 6-Hour Desk**: Automated statutory incident intimation countdown clock with notification dispatch for affected Data Principals.
  - **Nominee Claims Portal**: Public self-service flow for registered nominees to claim rights upon verified death/incapacity of a Data Principal.
</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-15" description="v1.0.0 — Privacy Vault & Multilingual Notices" tags={["Vault", "Notice"]}>
  ## Zero-Trust Vault & 22 Languages

  - **Zero-Trust Privacy Vault**: Field-level tokenization isolating personal identifiers from application relational databases.
  - **22 Scheduled Indian Languages**: Full translation and notice governance support across Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali, Marathi, and English.
  - **Subject Rights Requests (SRR)**: Automated intake for access, correction, and erasure with internal 72-hour SLAs.
</Update>
