The **Data Submissions** page (`/dpdpc/<org-slug>/data`) lists every form submission across the organization with parent/guardian details and per-purpose consent evidence — the record layer behind your forms.

## Browse submissions

- **Search** — find submissions by student/user, parent, email, or submission ID.
- **Form filter** — All forms, or a specific form.
- **Applicant filter** — All applicants, Child, or Adult.
- **Report scope** — org-wide users with multiple campuses can scope the audit pack to **Consolidated (All Branches)** or a single campus.
- **Table columns** — Submitted, Form, Student / User, Parent/Guardian, Signature, Consent (granted vs total purposes), and the submission ID.

Click any row to open the **Submission detail** drawer: identity and signing details, the **Consent Receipt** (with per-purpose Granted/Declined evidence and an integrity verification badge), file attachments with view-only secure previews, and the captured field evidence. Terms rows link to the signed agreement PDF.

<Note>
Viewing submissions is audit-logged — the drawer records that the record was inspected.
</Note>

## Download the audit pack

Click **Audit Pack PDF** for a board-ready PDF of the submissions (respects the form filter and report scope):

- Small packs download immediately.
- Large packs are generated in the background: you'll get an email with secure, time-limited links (packs are split into chunks of 500 records per PDF). When the list shows more than 400 rows, an **Email large pack** button queues the pack instead of downloading.

## Re-consent queue and campaigns

When a form's notice version changes, some principals' consents fall behind. With a specific form selected:

1. Click **Re-consent Queue** — a banner lists principals whose consent is on an **Outdated version** or **Missing required purposes**.
2. Click **Send Re-Consent Campaign** to open the **DPDP Sec 6(7) Re-Consent Campaign** dialog.
3. Edit the email subject and your message, then **Launch Email Blast**. Principals receive links to review the updated terms, and every dispatch is logged to the consent audit trail.

If everyone is current, the check reports that all consents are up to date.

## Erase a submission

In the submission drawer, the **Danger — Erasure** block offers **Delete Submission**. The confirmation explains that the record, its files in cloud storage, and access-log tokens are permanently purged — this cannot be undone.
