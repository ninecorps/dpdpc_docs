The **Incidents** desk (`/dpdpc/<org-slug>/incidents`) is your incident and breach response workspace. It handles two tracks: **Personal Data Breach** (DPDP statutory track with the 72-hour Data Protection Board reporting window under Section 8(6)) and **Others** (operational and physical events, tracked internally).

## The desk

- **KPIs** — Active 6h Timers, Active 72h Breaches, Affected People, Filings Completed.
- **Filter tabs** — All, Active Timers, DPDP Breaches, Critical P1, Statutory Filed, Drills.
- **Views** — a **Table view** or a **Kanban Board view** with lanes from Triage & Containment to Resolved & Closed.
- **Search** — by reference, summary, symptoms, or Board acknowledgement ID.

## Declare a personal data breach

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the wizard">
    Click **Declare Incident** and choose **Personal Data Breach** (badge: 72-Hour Board Intimation).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the breach">
    Fill the title, **Discovered At** time, location, data format (digital, digitised, or physical paper only — paper records are exempt under Section 3(a)), vendor if any, and the description with containment steps taken.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assess the scope">
    Select the **Compromised Data Categories** (Aadhaar / Govt ID, Fee / Bank Details, Health / Medical Records, and so on — or add your own), enter the **Estimated People Affected**, and review the auto-calculated **Harm Risk** badge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach evidence and assign an owner">
    Add up to 8 evidence files (10 MB each, 25 MB total). The assigned contact defaults to your organization's grievance officer and can be edited per incident.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Declare">
    Click **Declare Incident** — the 72-hour Board intimation window activates on declaration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Dispatch statutory notices

Declaring a breach does not email anyone by itself — notices wait for you:

1. The **Statutory notice dispatch** panel lists recipients staged in pending status. Review the recipient count and server pacing.
2. Click **Send all statutory notices**. Every affected principal receives a Rule 7(1) breach notice with the school's breach contact and grievance link. Watch the Delivered / Pending / Failed / Unreached counters until they settle.
3. From the incident dossier, **Send update** lets you notify everyone already notified about what has changed (for example, a revised record count).

<Tip>
Use **Mock Audit Drill** to rehearse the full flow — countdown, notices, and all — without sending real email. Drill incidents are clearly labelled and kept out of live filters.
</Tip>

## Declare other incidents

For operational and physical events (stolen hardware, facility intrusion, vendor outage, paper exposure), choose **Others (Operational / Physical)**, pick an incident category and severity, and list affected assets. Tick **This incident involved or exposed personal data** only if personal data was exposed — that activates the same Board countdown and notice flow.

## Work the dossier

Open any incident (row, card, or **Dossier**) to see severity, affected people, risk profile, leaked data categories, and the statutory obligations ladder: the DPBI first intimation (Rule 7(2)(a)), the detailed 72-hour report (Rule 7(2)(b)) with its deadline, and the parent/principal notices (Rule 7(1)). From here you can **Download report** (a consolidated PDF) and manage evidence files.

<Warning>
Deleting an incident permanently removes the incident, its statutory notices and filings, evidence files, and audit ledger. This cannot be undone.
</Warning>
