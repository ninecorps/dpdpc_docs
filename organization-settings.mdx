The Settings sidebar groups everything about your organization under **General**: Organization Details, Branches, Grievance Officer, Email Settings, and Danger Zone.

## Organization details

Configure your statutory legal identity: organization name (required), website, physical address, contact email, and contact phone. Upload your **Logo & Brand Mark** (under 1.5 MB) — it appears on public consent forms, the vault branding header, and audit-pack PDF letterheads.

<Note>
If your contact email uses a public domain (Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook, and similar), the platform sends via a protected relay address to keep deliverability high.
</Note>

## Grievance officer (DPDP Rule 9)

Every Data Fiduciary must publish grievance officer coordinates in privacy notices and consent requests. On the **Grievance Officer** page, enter the officer's name, email, and phone. The **Live Preview** card shows exactly what data principals will see, and **Open Vault to verify** lets you check the public portal. Once saved, the officer appears in public DPDP disclosures, consent revocation forms, and the compliance portal.

## Branches & campuses

Each branch inherits the master logo and can run its own local grievance desk:

- **Add Branch** — name (required), city, address shown on form footers, and a head officer who receives an email invitation to lead the branch.
- **Edit / GRO** — set the branch's local Grievance Redressal Officer (DPDP Sec 8(10)) who receives complaints for that campus.
- **Set Head Office** — one branch is the head office at a time.
- **Archive / Restore** — archived branches stay in the directory, dimmed.
- **Delete** — only empty branches (no members, invitations, or forms) can be deleted, and never the head office.

## Danger zone

Organization deletion permanently destroys all organizational data — forms, submissions, consent records, and audit logs. It is available only to organization-wide administrators with the delete permission.

<Warning>
Deletion requires typing the organization name plus a second identity confirmation (authenticator code or emailed code). Your **account is kept** — afterwards you land on the setup page and can create a new organization.
</Warning>
