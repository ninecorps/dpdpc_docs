Section 8(6) of the DPDP Act mandates that in the event of a personal data breach, the Data Fiduciary **must give intimation of such breach to the Data Protection Board and to each affected Data Principal** in the prescribed form and manner.

Additionally, under the **CERT-In Directions (2022)**, cybersecurity incidents must be reported to CERT-In within **6 hours** of notice or detection.

## Incident Desk Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Incident Intake & 6-Hour Countdown Initiation">
    When a security anomaly, unauthorized vault access, or data leakage is reported, an incident ticket is generated and the statutory 6-hour CERT-In countdown clock starts immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Containment & Scope Assessment">
    Identify affected vault partitions, tenant campuses, principal tokens, and field types compromised.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Regulatory Intimation (CERT-In & Data Protection Board)">
    Generate the standardized initial incident report containing attack vector, systems affected, timeline, and mitigation measures taken.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Data Principal Breach Notifications">
    Dispatch clear, unbundled notices to every affected Data Principal detailing the nature of the breach, compromised data categories, and recommended safety steps.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Severity Matrix & Reporting Timelines

| Severity Level | Criteria | Statutory Reporting Window | Principal Notification |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Critical (P0)** | Active exfiltration of biometric, financial, or minor child vault records | 6 Hours (CERT-In + Board) | Immediate (SMS + Email) |
| **High (P1)** | Unauthorized credential disclosure, isolated vault token exposure | 6 Hours (CERT-In) | Within 24 Hours |
| **Medium (P2)** | Denied intrusion attempt, brute force lockout anomaly | 24 Hours internal triage | Optional / Ad-hoc |
| **Low (P3)** | Low-risk configuration drift resolved without data exposure | Logged in periodic audit | Internal only |

<Warning>
  Failure to take reasonable security safeguards under Section 8(5) attracts statutory financial penalties up to **₹250 Crore**, and failure to intimate personal data breaches under Section 8(6) attracts penalties up to **₹200 Crore** (Section 33 Schedule).
</Warning>
